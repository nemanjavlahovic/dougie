# Cup artwork

`demo.gif` is rendered from the app by `scripts/make-demo.sh`. It shows a shortened timer and refill using the current interface; no third-party footage is included.

`Sources/Dougie/Assets/lludix-cup.png` is the transparent 1254 × 1254 source image used by `CoffeeCupView`. It was produced with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-16 using the user's chosen product as a design reference.

Reference: [SKLUM Lludix stainless-steel cup and saucer](https://www.sklum.com/es/comprar-tazas/223697-pack-de-2-tazas-de-cafe-17-cl-con-plato-en-acero-inoxidable-lludix.html?id_c=662144). The original retailer photos are reference inputs and are not bundled in the app.

The asset stays static in a `CALayer`. A separate coffee surface moves behind a fixed elliptical `CAShapeLayer` aperture. After filling, a radial reflection drifts slowly across the coffee, a small highlight ripples, and three feathered steam wisps rise, curl, and fade on staggered cycles. The mouth coordinates are registered to this image, so replacing it requires adjusting those coordinates. There is no 3D mesh, Metal shader, per-frame app render loop, or timer for the artwork. Reopening an active session shows a full cup and resumes ambient motion without restarting the session. Hidden or fully covered windows stop all animations. Reduce Motion keeps the coffee and steam static.

## Generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: high-resolution transparent PNG artwork for a small native macOS coffee utility, to be composited with an independently animated coffee surface. Reference images show the user's chosen SKLUM Lludix stainless-steel cup and saucer. Use the photos as object-design references. Create ONE single beautifully photographed empty cup on its matching saucer, preserving the reference's straight cylindrical thin-walled body, nearly circular flat ribbon handle attached on the RIGHT, fine rolled metallic rim, low circular pedestal foot, shallow flat round saucer. Keep the exact elegant industrial stainless-steel character. It is silver stainless steel, NOT copper, not gold, not ceramic, not a rounded teacup. Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background (alpha), no table, no backdrop, no text, no props, no second cup. Composition: square 1024x1024 asset; full cup and saucer centrally framed with 10% breathing room; orthographic-like studio product photograph, camera elevated about 30 degrees so the empty elliptical mouth and dark metal interior are clearly visible; cup and mouth symmetrical and level, ellipse horizontal; no camera roll. Material: premium mirror-polished stainless steel with nuanced broad warm-neutral studio reflections, one crisp softbox highlight on left and a deep graphite reflected band on the right, realistic subtle fine brushing, accurate edge thickness. Empty interior is clean silver with soft dark reflections, absolutely NO coffee, NO foam, NO steam. Saucer contact shadow is subtle, no cast shadow beyond saucer silhouette. Lighting: sophisticated Scandinavian product photography, beautiful controlled contrast, clean and sharply resolved at small UI sizes. Preserve realism; no illustrated vector look, no exaggerated bevels, no plastic CGI, no logos or watermark.
